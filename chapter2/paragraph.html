about mongo

Mango (Mangifera indica), often called the "king of fruits," is a tropical drupe known for its juicy, sweet, and fibrous flesh, cultivated widely for its rich flavor and high vitamin A and C content. Primarily grown in India, mangoes are used in desserts, drinks like lassi, and savory dishes like pickles

types of mangoes

Mangoes come in hundreds of varieties with distinct tastes, shapes, and colors, often categorized by origin and texture. Key types include the sweet, fiber-free Indian Alphonso, aromatic Kesar, and long, sweet Dasheri. Popular international types include the orange-fleshed Manila and mild, firm Palmer
